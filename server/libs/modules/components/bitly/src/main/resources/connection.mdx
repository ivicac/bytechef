## Connection Setup

### Create Bitly Access Token

1. Log in to your [Bitly account](https://app.bitly.com/).
2. Click on your account name in the bottom left corner and select **Settings**.
3. Open the **API** section under **Developer settings**.
4. Under **Access token**, enter your account password and click **Generate token**.
5. Copy the generated access token and paste it into the connection.
