If you're shipping a SaaS product, your customers are going to ask for integrations: "Can you connect to Salesforce? Slack? Our internal CRM? Our data warehouse?" The traditional answers are:

1. **Build the integration team.** Slow, expensive, never-ending.
2. **Pick one or two integrations.** Customers churn over the ones you skipped.
3. **Embed an iPaaS.** Get hundreds of integrations and a workflow builder, branded as yours.

ByteChef's Embedded iPaaS is option 3 — and because the platform is source-available, you keep control of the engine.

## What you get

| Capability | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [White-label execution](/enterprise/embedded-ipaas/white-label-execution) | The workflow engine, runs inside your product, branded as yours. |
| [Embedded configuration API](/enterprise/embedded-ipaas/configuration-api) | Programmatic CRUD for projects, workflows, and connections — designed for product surfaces. |
| [Permission expressions](/enterprise/embedded-ipaas/permission-expressions) | Gate which integrations and workflows each connected user sees, driven by their plan, region, or feature flags. |
| [Tenant-isolated security](/enterprise/embedded-ipaas/tenant-isolated-security) | Multi-tenant defaults that fail safe. |
| [Embedded MCP](/enterprise/embedded-ipaas/embedded-mcp) | Surface each tenant's workflows as MCP tools for their agents. |

## The mental model

```
       Your SaaS product
       ┌────────────────────────────────┐
       │  Your UI / Your branding       │
       │  ┌──────────────────────────┐  │
       │  │  ByteChef-rendered       │  │
       │  │  workflow builder        │  │  ◄── embedded via iframe / SDK
       │  │  (white-labeled)         │  │
       │  └──────────────────────────┘  │
       │  ┌──────────────────────────┐  │
       │  │  Your business logic     │  │
       │  └──────────────────────────┘  │
       └────────────────────────────────┘
                      │
                      ▼
       ┌────────────────────────────────┐
       │  ByteChef Enterprise           │
       │  ┌──────────────┐  ┌────────┐  │
       │  │ Tenant A     │  │ Tenant │  │
       │  │ workspace    │  │   B    │  │  ◄── per-customer isolation
       │  └──────────────┘  └────────┘  │
       └────────────────────────────────┘
```

Your customer never sees "ByteChef." They see your product offering integrations.

## The promise to your customers

Embedded gives your customers:

- **Hundreds of integrations** without you having to build them.
- **A visual workflow builder** they can use without writing code.
- **Their own connections** — they bring their own credentials; you don't see them.
- **Their data, isolated** — your other customers can't see their workflows or data.

## The promise to you

- **No integration team.** ByteChef ships and maintains the [180+ built-in components](/enterprise/extensibility/built-in-components).
- **No new infrastructure to operate** — Embedded is the same binary you've already deployed.
- **One billing surface.** You charge your customers; ByteChef bills you.
- **Source-available engine.** No vendor lock-in on the part of your product that powers a critical workflow surface.
