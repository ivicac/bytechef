# ByteChef Automation (Track A) — Croatia Target List (first cut, expanded)

**Date:** October 4, 2026 · **Companies:** 114 internal-use buyers (target 100) + 27 implementation partners · **Purpose:** GTM strategy §6, Track A (OSS Automation). These are Croatian companies that would run ByteChef for their own internal automation and AI agents, plus the agencies that could implement it for many such companies. The embedded (Track B) Croatia segment lives in `bytechef-embedded-target-list-2026-07.md`.

## How this list was built

Three parallel research passes against the §6.1 ICP:
1. Companies with **direct automation-tooling signals**: job ads, posts or talks naming Zapier, Make, n8n, Power Automate, RPA or AI-agent work.
2. **Self-host and regulated** mid-size organisations: fintech, banking, crypto, energy, healthcare, Java/Spring shops, and anyone under DORA, NIS2 or MiCA.
3. **Implementation partners**: Croatian agencies, consultancies and integrators that already sell automation work.

A second round (same day) widened the buyer search to 100 with sector passes: tech and product companies, e-commerce and retail, tourism and hospitality, manufacturing/energy/logistics, finance/insurance/healthcare, media/telecom/services, utilities/public companies/food/education, plus a top-up of leftovers and a second partner pass (Odoo, Red Hat, Salesforce, Dynamics and Bitrix24 partners). Headcounts come mostly from Fina InfoBiz, CompanyWall and reputacija.hr (2025 filings); companies over 500 by FINA headcount were dropped, and every company appears once.

ICP (§6.1): 10–500 people with engineering capability; a preference for self-hosting (data control, EU compliance, predictable cost); currently on Zapier/Make and hitting per-task price walls, or outgrowing n8n; teams standardising AI agents that need guardrails and human-in-the-loop. Excluded: companies over 500 people, automation/iPaaS/RPA vendors (competitors), and, from the buyer list, agencies (they are scored as partners).

**Buyer rubric (/10):** Size fit 0–2 · Engineering capability 0–2 (Java/Spring stack = 2) · Automation signal 0–3 (3 = a named Zapier/Make/n8n/Power Automate tool or an automation hire) · Self-host / compliance driver 0–2 · AI-agent signal +1.

**Partner rubric (/10):** Automation-practice evidence 0–3 · Open-source / self-host affinity 0–2 · Client-base fit (Croatian/EU SMB and mid-market) 0–2 · Size/reach 0–2 · Java/Spring or AI-agent practice +1.

**Read before sending anything:**
1. Track A is an **inbound-led** motion in the strategy. This list is for selective, founder-led outreach in the home market, aimed at first payers and early production references, not a volume sequence. Keep it below the Track B sprint in priority until the Day-90 gate.
2. Coverage is partial. The tooling-signal pass hit its search cap and LinkedIn rate limits; Croatian job boards are poorly indexed by search; and the Make, Zapier and n8n partner directories could not be filtered by country. Treat this as a first cut and do a manual sweep (see *Research gaps*).
3. Many signals are **job ads**. Ads close and roles change, so re-open each one before citing it. Hooks that reference a posting should say "saw you were hiring for…", not "you use…".
4. Regulated buyers (banks, HBOR, JANAF, ZSE) have long cycles and public-procurement rules. They are good self-host proof points, but slow to become first payers.
5. Pitch in Croatian. "Open source" is literally true for Track A, so say it plainly (strategy §3.2).

## Suggested sequencing

1. **Partners first.** One agency that already sells Make or n8n work reaches dozens of SMBs. Start with CROZ, Kodilion, Notch and Provexio; treat the micro n8n shops (Sinqra, AIS Tech, cerkicai) as quick design-partner conversations; and run the Odoo partners as one mini-campaign with one pitch ("the integration layer around Odoo").
2. **Named-tool users next.** Companies with budget and intent today: Alma Career Croatia (an Internal Tools Engineer for M365/Slack/Power Platform automation and AI in IT ops), Plavi tim (INA's IT company, 100+ UiPath robots), FORA Solutions (a payments-support lead to cut manual work), PREMIS (n8n/Make/Zapier in its job ad), A.G.S. Zagreb (heavy Power Automate use), GA Croatia (a Power Platform process-automation hire), Hypefy (AI Enablement building workflow agents), AGRIVI (AI & Automation Engineer), Hidrocibalae (200+ Python automations and a new Head of Technology), Zagrel Rittmeyer (PowerShell/Python process automation), and MTS online (n8n in its stack, under the size floor).
3. **Regulated self-hosters in parallel, as slow burners.** Lead with self-hosting inside the DORA/NIS2/MiCA perimeter, AI agents with human approval, and no per-task fees:
   - finance: Aircash, HBOR, KentBank, J&T banka, Solvera, HOK, Erste Group Card Processor, Identyum
   - water utilities: VIK Split, Međimurske vode, Vodovod-Osijek, Koprivničke vode
   - energy and ports: Plinacro, Luka Ploče, AGCT
4. **One account, several companies.** Poslovni sustavi (Rijeka) runs IT for five city companies. Agram Life and Specijalna bolnica AGRAM share Agram-group IT. Angelina Yachting and Ultra Sailing share a board. Approach each group once.
