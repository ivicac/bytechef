# ByteChef Embedded — Outbound Target List (first cut)

**Date:** July 28, 2026 (Croatia segment added October 4, 2026) · **Companies:** 101 across 5 segments · **Purpose:** GTM strategy §7.3 — the 90-day embedded validation sprint's outreach list.

## How this list was built

Four parallel research passes (EU vertical SaaS · US/RoW vertical SaaS · dev tools & horizontal B2B · HR/fin-ops/accounting) against the §7.3 criteria: B2B SaaS, seed–Series B, ~10–200 employees, demonstrated integration pain, verticals whose customers demand integrations. Competitors (iPaaS / unified-API / workflow-automation vendors) and Series C+ / >300-employee companies were excluded.

**Scoring rubric (per company, /10):** Stage fit 0–2 · Size fit 0–2 · Pain evidence 0–3 · Vertical fit 0–2 · EU bonus +1 (applied to European HQs incl. UK — deduct 1 for strict EU-27).

**Global caveats — read before sending anything:**
1. Most companies' `/integrations` pages blocked automated fetching (403). "Thin page" judgments rest on review sites, support docs, changelogs, and search-indexed copy. **Verify each company's current integrations page manually before citing it in an email.**
2. Funding/headcount figures marked with aggregator sources (Tracxn, PitchBook, Getlatka, LeadIQ) are directional. Verify stage and size on LinkedIn before sequencing — a few entries may have outgrown the band (Otto, HERO, SPRY flagged in-segment).
3. Each segment ends with its own low-confidence list. Treat those rows as "verify first," not "send first."
4. Every row includes a suggested first-line hook referencing its specific evidence — rewrite in your own voice; never send a hook whose underlying fact you haven't re-verified (a wrong claim about their product kills the thread).

# Segment: Croatia (28 companies)

**Added:** October 4, 2026, from three parallel research passes: Croatian vertical SaaS · Croatian horizontal B2B, HR and fin-ops SaaS · the Fiskalizacija 2.0 e-invoicing and SMB-ERP market. Selection criteria and exclusions are the same as the rest of the list. Scoring uses the same rubric: Stage 0–2, Size 0–2, Pain 0–3, Vertical 0–2, EU +1. Croatia is in the EU-27, so a Croatian HQ earns the +1 without the UK caveat. OptimoRoute (US HQ) and Ascalia (UK HQ) do not get it.

**Why Croatia is its own segment:** a home-market, same-language channel; warm-intro density (Infinum, Fil Rouge, SCV, AYMO portfolios); and a regulatory deadline that creates integration pain on a date:

> **Fiskalizacija 2.0.** Since 1 January 2026, every Croatian VAT-registered business must issue and receive e-invoices through an information intermediary chosen in FiskAplikacija ([Porezna leaflet](https://porezna-uprava.gov.hr/hr/fiskalizacija-2-0-letak/8083)). **From 1 January 2027** the obligation extends to businesses outside the VAT system, including paušalisti ([Parra](https://www.parra.hr/eracuni-poduzetnici-izvan-pdv-sustava-2027/), [Arges](https://arges.hr/blog/eracuni-2027-izvan-pdv-a/)), so **Q4 2026 is the deadline window** for every invoicing and accounting vendor serving micro-businesses. The official register already lists **34 certified intermediaries, each with its own API** ([Porezna list](https://porezna-uprava.gov.hr/hr/popis-informacijskih-posrednika/8019)). In a February 2026 Competition Agency decision, mer states that each ERP↔intermediary integration takes **3–6 months**, and ERPs therefore integrate only a few intermediaries ([AZTN decision](https://www.aztn.hr/ea/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/UPI-034-032025-03021.pdf)). That is the pitch in one line: a deadline, 34 APIs, and months per hand-built connector.

**Croatia wave plan:** Wave 1 (score ≥9) is 14 companies: Rentlio, Tomsoft/Luceed, Productive, Farseer, Lemax, AGRIVI, Nokumo, Mireo, DotYourSpot, Micronic, E-RAČUNI, ViziBit, TalentLyft, Oradian. The Fiskalizacija sub-segment should go first regardless of score, because the 2027 wave makes Q4 2026 the moment.

**Caveats specific to this segment:**
1. Many headcounts come from Fina InfoBiz annual filings (2025). These are more reliable than the aggregator estimates used elsewhere in this list, but they lag by a year.
2. Several Fiskalizacija vendors are long-running, founder-owned private SMBs rather than venture-backed startups. "Stage fit" was scored as bootstrapped/private = 2. Expect a director-level buyer and a slower, relationship-led sale.
3. Pitch in Croatian. Swap "Zapier" for "Make" or "CSV export" where that is the local reality.
